# Code of Conduct

## Our Standard

We aim for a respectful, constructive, and inclusive collaboration space.
Participants are expected to communicate in good faith, welcome different
perspectives, give actionable feedback, and respect reasonable boundaries.

Harassment, personal attacks, threats, discriminatory language, deliberate
disruption, and sharing another person's private information are not welcome.

## Enforcement

Repository maintainers may remove, edit, or reject contributions and take any
other action they consider appropriate for conduct that violates this standard.

To report a concern, contact a maintainer privately through a contact channel
listed on their GitHub profile. Reports will be handled as discreetly as the
available information allows.
